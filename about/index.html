About TasteRank

TasteRank is a game for ranking the creative people you love: film directors, musicians, writers and artists. You pick one of two, and we sort your answers into your personal Top 9. You can share the result and challenge friends to see how your tastes match.

Where the data comes from

People and facts come from Wikidata (CC0). Lists are ordered by how many Wikipedia language editions cover each person.

Photo credits

All photos come from Wikimedia Commons and are used under their free licenses (such as CC BY, CC BY-SA or public domain). The author and license of each photo are shown on its card during the game. We never use photos from Google Images, IMDb or other non-free sources. People without a free photo appear as an initials card.

Takedowns and corrections

Names, facts and photos come from Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons. To correct an entry or a photo, edit it there (anyone can); the fix appears here the next time the lists are refreshed.
