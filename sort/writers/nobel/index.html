Frequently asked questions

What is the Nobel Prize in Literature Winners Top 9?
A game where you compare writers two at a time and pick the one you like more. Your answers are sorted into your personal Top 9.
How does it work?
1. Choose Standard or Expanded. 2. Tap the faces you like, then pick one of two in each duel. 3. See your Top 9 and share it.
Can I compare with friends?
Yes. After your Top 9, copy the challenge link. Your friend makes theirs and you both see a taste-match score.

About these people and photos

The list is built from Wikidata and ordered by how widely each person is covered on Wikipedia. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licenses; the author and license are shown on each card. People without a free photo get a coloured initials card.
